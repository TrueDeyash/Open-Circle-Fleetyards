Our History

Fourteen years after the fall of the Republic, our founder, a veteran clone trooper finally hung up his blaster for good. After years of mercenary work in the chaotic early Imperial era, he used his savings and hard-won knowledge of military hardware to found Open Circle Shipyards, named after an old republic What began as a modest repair yard has since grown into a full production facility capable of building everything from classic Venator-class designs to Imperial Star Destroyers, cruisers, and starfighters.

Situated in the orbit of mandalore, this colossal shipyards can create entire battle fleets from scratch whithin hours that are immediately ready for battle, with modules sourced from all corners of the galaxy our shipyards offer the most versatility and customisability, want to equip your star destroyer with top of the line mandalorian weapons and/or Mon cala shielding? look no further.
